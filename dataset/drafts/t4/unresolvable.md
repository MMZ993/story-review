# unresolvable — T4 draft

## T4 rendering

**Title:** As an international customer, I want checkout localized so that checkout is usable for me.

**Description:**

Checkout is English-only, EUR-only, and offers cards and PayPal only. The shop already ships to several EU markets, but checkout has not followed the international expansion. Sales wants five additional markets live this year, and support reports that non-English tickets already take days to resolve.

Finance has no position on display currencies and FX pricing, and there is no single owner for international checkout. The scope is not defined: markets, languages, currencies, and local payment methods are all open.

**Acceptance Criteria field:**

- When an international customer reaches checkout, checkout is usable for them.
- When prices are displayed in a non-EUR market, they are correct for that market.
- When an international order is placed, it can be fulfilled like a domestic one.
