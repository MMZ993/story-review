Sales wants five additional markets live this year, support says non-English tickets already take days to resolve, finance has no position on display currencies and FX pricing, and there is no single owner for international checkout. Scope is not defined: markets, languages, currencies, and local payment methods are all open.

When an international customer reaches checkout, checkout is usable for them. When prices are displayed in a non-EUR market, they are correct for that market. When an international order is placed, it can be fulfilled like a domestic one.
