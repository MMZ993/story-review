Title: Localize checkout for international customers

Description:
When international customers reach an English-only, EUR-only checkout that offers cards and PayPal only, I want checkout localized for international customers, so I can support the shop's international expansion.

The shop already ships to several EU markets; checkout has not followed the international expansion. Sales wants five additional markets live this year. Support reports non-English tickets already take days to resolve. Finance has no position on display currencies and FX pricing, and there is no single owner for international checkout.

Scope is not defined: markets, languages, currencies, and local payment methods are all open.

Acceptance Criteria field:
- When an international customer reaches checkout, checkout is usable for them.
- When prices are displayed in a non-EUR market, they are correct for that market.
- When an international order is placed, it can be fulfilled like a domestic one.
